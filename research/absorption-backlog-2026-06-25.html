Top recommendation

  1. Ship the lint-gated edit hook first (#1). It's an S-effort PostToolUse hook that slots beside the existing apex-subagent-driven-law.sh, reuses phantom's hook plumbing, and has the strongest published ROI of anything surveyed — it stops bad edits at the source instead of catching them three loops later in verify.
  2. Then do the context-budget trio: scored retrieval (#2), trigger-gated learnings (#3), glob-scoped attach (#5). All three plug into memory-reader.js / _shared-auto-learning.md, and together they replace "dump the whole domain file" with "load the few learnings that actually matter here" — the highest-leverage, lowest-risk cluster, and squarely aligned with Less is More.
  3. Hold the line on everything in the reject table. The heavy ideas (time-travel checkpoints, event-stream rearchitecture, consensus voting, blackboard, DSPy compile) are speculative for phantom's observed failure modes — adopt only when a concrete recurring failure demands them, not because a famous framework has them.
