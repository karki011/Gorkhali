# Engineering conventions

Gorkhali supports Claude Code. The orchestrator plans and coordinates; Engineer
implements; Inspector checks; Auditor independently reviews. Opposition and
Detective are conditional specialists. Use `/gorkhali:start` for new work.

Document repository-specific commands, ownership boundaries, and constraints here.
Prefer today's actual requirements, meaningful small interfaces, explicit dependencies,
behavioral tests, and reversible choices. Do not copy model IDs or routing thresholds
from the installed plugin: its policy files own them.
